Anuj Sengar (AJ)

Founder, RCIC

Anuj Sengar (AJ)

Founder, RCIC

Anuj’s story is not one of privilege, but of perseverance. He grew up in India with dreams bigger than his surroundings, carrying the quiet determination that education could be his bridge to something greater. After completing his Master’s at Punjab Agricultural University, India’s top agricultural university, he secured his first role in Canada through a campus placement program. That opportunity opened a door, not just to a career, but to an understanding: recruitment has the power to change lives and help companies thrive. But while a job gave him a beginning, immigration tested him deeply. Alone in a new country, with no professional to guide him, Anuj took on every form, every rule, every sleepless night of uncertainty by himself. He still remembers spreading papers across his small basement, double checking every detail, knowing one mistake could cost him everything. It was overwhelming, yet it forged in him a rare strength: the lived understanding of what it means to fight for both opportunity and belonging. That dual experience became the foundation of Can X. As its Founder, Anuj built the company with a simple but powerful vision: “We help Companies Thrive, People Succeed!” Through recruitment, Anuj has been helping businesses of all sizes solve their labour shortages across construction, manufacturing, retail, and healthcare. He believes that every successful brand once began as a small company, just as Can X did, and for this reason, his commitment is to support both small businesses and large enterprises alike. Whether it’s finding skilled tradespeople for a local contractor or connecting global talent with some of Canada’s most recognized brands, Anuj sees recruitment not just as filling jobs, but as building futures. Clients describe Anuj as both meticulous and deeply human, a professional who simplifies what feels impossible, and a guide who carries their anxieties as if they were his own. Under his leadership, Can X has touched lives in 30+ countries, creating not just files and approvals, but futures filled with stability, dignity, and hope. Beyond his professional life, Anuj is a husband and father who finds joy in cultivating balance. He is an advocate of organic farming, growing his own organic vegetables in his garden, a practice that reflects his belief in nurturing life from the ground up. He loves the quiet strategy of chess, the endless learning from reading books, and the peace of exploring nature. These passions ground him, reminding him that true growth, whether in people, companies, or crops, comes with patience, care, and vision. For Anuj, success is not measured by numbers or titles. It is measured in the silent relief of a parent who can finally hold their child again, the pride of a worker stepping into their first Canadian job, and the gratitude of an employer whose team is complete. And that is why his story matters, because at Can X, every success is not just a transaction. It is a transformation.
  • Email:anuj@canxglobal.com

Amanpreet Singh

Vice President

Amanpreet Singh

Vice President

Amanpreet’s story is one of determination, growth, and balance, reflecting the very mission of Can X. As Vice President, he plays a pivotal role in helping Canadian businesses address labour shortages through recruitment while also guiding individuals on their immigration journeys. His strength lies in understanding both sides of the process: what companies need to thrive and what people need to succeed. Originally from India, Amanpreet completed a Bachelor’s degree in Engineering. After moving to Canada, he pursued a Post-Graduate Diploma in Business from Douglas College. While still a student, he began his career with Can X as a Sales Executive. His dedication, results, and client-focused approach quickly set him apart, leading to promotions first as Manager, then Sales Director and later as Vice President. His own professional journey mirrors the opportunities he now creates for others. Recruitment has been at the heart of Amanpreet’s career. He has worked with leading Canadian brands such as Dairy Queen, Tim Hortons, Subway, McDonald’s, Fresh Slice Pizza, Freshii, Husky, Canco, Jugo Juice, Petro Canada, Mr. Lube, Panago, Guardtek, Shoppers Drug Mart, Super Save Gas, Browns Social House, DeDutch, and Freedom Mobile. At the same time, he has partnered with countless small businesses in construction, retail, and healthcare, companies that form the backbone of local communities. His philosophy is simple: every big brand once started small, just as Can X did. That is why he is equally committed to supporting startups looking for their first hires and established enterprises seeking global talent. For Amanpreet, recruitment is not just about filling jobs; it is about fueling growth and creating life-changing opportunities. His own immigration journey gave him empathy and insight into the challenges newcomers face. He understands the uncertainty of navigating a new system, and that perspective shapes his client-first approach. For him, success is measured not just by contracts signed or deals closed, but by futures secured. As Vice President, Amanpreet combines the precision of an engineer with the vision of a strategist. He leads a dynamic sales team, fosters innovation, and builds long-term partnerships rooted in trust. Under his leadership, Can X has expanded its reach across Canada, strengthening the company’s dual mission of recruitment and immigration. Outside of work, Amanpreet is a fitness enthusiast who values a healthy lifestyle. He enjoys outdoor adventures, staying active, and spending quality time with family and friends. This balance gives him the energy to continue building bridges between companies and talent, and between dreams and opportunities. For Amanpreet, every successful hire and every approved application reflects the essence of Can X’s vision: We help Companies Thrive, People Succeed!
