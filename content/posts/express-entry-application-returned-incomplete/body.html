There is a difference between a refused application and a returned one. A refusal means an officer assessed your application and decided against you. A return, often called an “R10 rejection” after section 10 of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Regulations (IRPR), means your application never made it to a full assessment because it was incomplete. For Express Entry applicants, a return can be devastating: you lose your ITA and must wait for a new invitation, possibly at a higher CRS cutoff.

This guide explains the legal rules on completeness, the most common reasons Express Entry applications are returned and how to submit a complete application the first time.

Quick Summary

Under sections 10 and 12 of the IRPR, an application that does not include the required forms, signatures, information, documents and fees is returned to the applicant. Express Entry applications go through a completeness check. Common causes of return include missing or invalid police certificates and, since October 21, 2025, missing immigration medical exam information. Because a returned Express Entry application does not restore your ITA, you must re-enter the pool and wait for a new invitation.

The Most Common Reasons for Return

Missing or invalid police certificates

Police certificates are frequently reported as the leading cause of incomplete Express Entry applications. You generally need a certificate for each country where you or your family members aged 18 or older lived for six months or more in a row since age 18. Problems include:

  • Certificates missing for one country.
  • Certificates issued before key dates, such as before you left the country.
  • Certificates that do not cover all names or aliases.
  • Certificates for current residence that are too old.

If you have requested a certificate but cannot get it in time, upload proof that you applied, along with an explanation.

Missing upfront medical exam information

According to IRCC, Express Entry applicants have been required to submit an immigration medical exam with their application since August 21, 2025. After a facilitation period ended on October 21, 2025, applications received without immigration medical exam information may be rejected. Book your exam with an IRCC panel physician as soon as you receive your ITA, or earlier if you are confident of an invitation.

Missing documents from your personalized checklist

Your document checklist is generated from your answers. Every item listed is required unless you upload an explanation where permitted.

Unsigned or incomplete forms

Missing signatures, blank mandatory fields and missing family information can lead to return.

Missing fees

Your application must include payment of the required fees.

Translation problems

Documents in languages other than English or French must be accompanied by translations, generally with a translator’s affidavit where the translator is not certified.

The Real Cost of a Return

When an Express Entry application is returned:

  • Your ITA is not restored.
  • You must have a valid profile and receive a new invitation.
  • CRS cutoffs may be higher when you are invited again.
  • Your age, language test validity or other factors may change, lowering your score.

How to Submit a Complete Application

  • Start police certificates before or immediately after your ITA.
  • Book your medical exam promptly.
  • Download and follow your personalized checklist item by item.
  • Check every form for signatures and complete answers.
  • Use explanation letters where a document is unavailable, and include proof of your efforts.
  • Review the full application before submitting; you cannot add missing elements after submission to cure a completeness problem.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an R10 rejection in Express Entry?

An R10 rejection refers to an application returned as incomplete because it did not meet the requirements of section 10 of the IRPR, such as required forms, signatures, information, documents or fees. Under section 12, the application is returned. For Express Entry, this means losing your ITA and waiting for a new invitation.

Do I need a medical exam before submitting my Express Entry application?

Yes. According to IRCC, Express Entry applicants have been required to submit an immigration medical exam with their application since August 21, 2025. Since October 21, 2025, applications received without medical exam information may be rejected. Book your exam with an IRCC panel physician promptly after your ITA.

What if I cannot get a police certificate in time?

Upload proof that you applied for the certificate, such as a receipt or confirmation, along with a letter explaining the delay. Requirements and processing times vary by country, so start early. Missing police certificates without proof of your efforts are a common reason applications are returned as incomplete.

Is a returned application the same as a refusal?

No. A returned application was not fully assessed because it was incomplete, and fees are generally refunded. A refusal means an officer assessed your application and decided you did not meet the requirements. In both cases, Express Entry applicants must receive a new ITA to apply again.
