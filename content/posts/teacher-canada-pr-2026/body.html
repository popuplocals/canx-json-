Quick Summary

Education is an official 2026 Express Entry category, but no education draws were held by early October 2026. British Columbia’s “Care” priority includes early childhood educators and French-speaking K-12 teachers, and Ontario lists early childhood educators as a priority occupation.

The Education Category

When IRCC launched the education category in 2025, it covered occupations such as elementary and secondary school teachers, early childhood educators and assistants, instructors of persons with disabilities and teacher assistants. To qualify for an education draw in 2026, candidates need at least 12 months of experience in an eligible occupation within the past three years and eligibility for an Express Entry program. Check IRCC’s current category list before relying on it, because category lists can change each year.

How Education Workers Compete in Express Entry

Without education draws, teachers rely on their Comprehensive Ranking System score in Canadian Experience Class or general rounds, or on other categories such as French. Strong language scores, Canadian experience and a second credential improve your score.

Teacher Certification in Canada

Teaching in public schools is regulated by each province. To teach, you usually need certification from the provincial teacher regulator, which reviews your education, teacher training, experience and language ability. Early childhood educators also need provincial certification in most provinces. Start this process early, as it can take months.

The French Advantage for Teachers

French-speaking teachers are in high demand across Canada, especially for French immersion and francophone schools outside Quebec. French at NCLC 7 or higher also qualifies you for French-language Express Entry draws, which carried some of the lowest cut-offs in 2026, and can add up to 50 additional CRS points.

Early Childhood Educators: A Strong Niche

Early childhood educators appear in several provincial priority lists in 2026. Many work in licensed child care centres that can offer job support. Certification, language scores and Canadian experience all strengthen applications.

Are there Express Entry draws for teachers in 2026?

Can I teach in Canada with a foreign teaching degree?

Possibly, but you usually need certification from the provincial teacher regulator before teaching in public schools. The regulator assesses your education, teacher training, experience and language ability. Requirements vary by province, and the process can take time, so start early.

Which provinces need teachers and early childhood educators?

In 2026, British Columbia’s “Care” priority includes early childhood educators and French-speaking K-12 teachers, and Ontario lists early childhood educators as a priority occupation under its Workforce Priority Stream. Other provinces may accept education workers with job offers or local experience.

Do French-speaking teachers have an advantage?

Yes. French-speaking teachers are in demand for French immersion and francophone schools outside Quebec. French at NCLC 7 or higher also qualifies candidates for French-language Express Entry draws, which had some of the lowest cut-offs in 2026, and can add up to 50 additional CRS points.
