Before March 25, 2025, a valid job offer could add 50 or 200 points to an Express Entry profile. Overnight, IRCC removed those points for more than 60,000 candidates, according to an Access to Information request reported by industry media. Many people still believe a job offer guarantees a higher CRS score 2026. It does not. But a Canadian job offer still matters, sometimes a great deal.

This guide explains exactly what changed, where a job offer still helps in Express Entry Canada 2026, and what IRCC’s proposed reforms could mean for job offer points in the future.

Quick Summary

Since March 25, 2025, IRCC awards no CRS points for job offers, including LMIA-supported offers. A valid job offer still matters for eligibility under the Federal Skilled Trades Program, can help with the Federal Skilled Worker 67-point grid, can exempt certain candidates from proof of funds, and opens doors to many Provincial Nominee Program streams worth 600 CRS points. IRCC has proposed reintroducing limited job offer points for high-wage occupations, but no change was in effect as of early October 2026.

What Changed in March 2025

IRCC confirmed on its official CRS criteria page that it no longer gives points for job offers. Previously, a qualifying offer in Major Group 00 (senior management) earned 200 points, and an offer in any other skilled occupation earned 50 points. The change applied to all current and future candidates in the pool, not just new profiles.

IRCC linked the decision to integrity concerns, including fraudulent or purchased Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) arrangements. The removal sharply reduced the number of profiles in the 500 to 600 score range in the weeks that followed, according to analysis of IRCC pool data.

Where a Job Offer Still Helps in 2026

1. Federal Skilled Trades Eligibility

To qualify for the Federal Skilled Trades Program, you need either a valid job offer of at least one year of full-time work (from up to two employers) or a certificate of qualification from a Canadian provincial or territorial authority. For many tradespeople, the job offer remains the gateway into Express Entry itself.

2. The Federal Skilled Worker 67-Point Grid

Federal Skilled Worker candidates must score at least 67 points on a separate selection grid before entering the pool. Arranged employment can contribute points on that grid, and IRCC has stated that job offers remain part of eligibility criteria. For borderline candidates, this can decide whether they qualify at all.

3. Proof of Funds Exemption

Express Entry candidates under FSW or FST normally must show settlement funds. You do not need to show funds if you are currently authorized to work in Canada and have a valid job offer. For families, that exemption can remove a significant financial hurdle.

4. Provincial Nominee Program Streams

Many PNP streams require or favour a Canadian job offer. An Express Entry-aligned nomination adds 600 CRS points, far more than the old 50-point job offer bonus ever did. In practice, a job offer often works best today as a route to a nomination.

5. Category Eligibility and Canadian Experience

A job offer that comes with a work permit lets you build Canadian work experience, which earns core and transferability points and may qualify you for the Canadian Experience Class and categories such as senior managers with Canadian work experience.

What Counts as a Valid Job Offer?

For Express Entry purposes, a valid job offer generally must be full-time, non-seasonal, for at least one year, in a skilled occupation (TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3 under the National Occupational Classification, or NOC codes), and supported by a positive LMIA or an LMIA-exempt work permit in specific cases. Federal Skilled Trades offers must be in an eligible trade. Always confirm the precise criteria on Canada.ca.

Watch Out for Job Offer Fraud

The removal of job offer points did not end job offer fraud. Some people still sell “guaranteed” job offers or LMIAs. Paying for a job offer or an LMIA is illegal for employers and can lead to refusal and misrepresentation findings for applicants. A genuine employer does not charge you for a job.

PNP vs Express Entry Canada: Using Your Job Offer Wisely

If you hold a genuine job offer, compare your options. Your offer may make you eligible for an employer-driven PNP stream, which could add 600 points to your Express Entry profile. In other cases, a base PNP stream outside Express Entry may suit you better. Each province sets its own rules, so map your offer against current streams.

What IRCC Has Proposed for Job Offer Points

In its 2026 to 2027 Departmental Plan and April 2026 consultations, IRCC proposed a “high-wage occupation” factor. Under the proposal, candidates with Canadian work experience or a job offer in occupations paying above set multiples of the national median wage would earn extra points. This would bring back job offer points, but only for high-wage roles.

Policy Watch

As of early October 2026, the high-wage occupation factor and any return of job offer points remained proposals. IRCC said CRS changes would come through Ministerial Instructions and could arrive faster than program changes. Do not tell readers points have returned until IRCC confirms it on Canada.ca.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do job offers give CRS points in 2026?

No. IRCC removed CRS points for job offers on March 25, 2025, including LMIA-supported offers and senior management offers. This applies to all candidates in the pool. IRCC has proposed reintroducing points for high-wage occupations, but as of early October 2026, that change had not taken effect. Check Canada.ca for current rules.

Is a job offer still useful for Express Entry?

Yes. A valid job offer can make you eligible for the Federal Skilled Trades Program, help you meet the Federal Skilled Worker 67-point grid, exempt you from proof of funds if you are authorized to work in Canada, and qualify you for many PNP streams that add 600 CRS points.

Should I keep my job offer in my Express Entry profile?

Yes. IRCC advises candidates to continue including valid job offer details in their profile because a job offer may remain part of eligibility for some programs, including the Federal Skilled Trades Program and certain PNP streams. Make sure every detail matches your offer letter and supporting documents.

Can I buy an LMIA or job offer to improve my chances?

No. Paying for a job offer or LMIA is illegal and can lead to refused applications, misrepresentation findings and a five-year ban. Genuine employers do not charge candidates for jobs. If someone offers to sell you a job offer, walk away and report it.
