Moving from the UK to Canada is easier than many Brits expect. UK citizens can visit with an Electronic Travel Authorization, young Brits can work through International Experience Canada for up to two years, and skilled professionals score well in Express Entry thanks to native English and strong qualifications. The challenge is choosing the right pathway and sequencing it well.

This guide explains how to move to Canada from the UK in 2026: temporary options such as IEC and work permits, permanent residence routes, practical documents and a step-by-step plan.

Quick Summary

UK citizens are visa-exempt and need an Electronic Travel Authorization to fly to Canada. Brits aged 18 to 35 can apply for International Experience Canada, with permits of up to two years and up to two participations. Skilled workers can apply for permanent residence through Express Entry or provincial nominee programs. Employer-supported work permits, intra-company transfers and trade agreement categories offer additional options.

Visiting Canada From the UK

UK citizens do not need a visitor visa, but they need an Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) to fly to or transit through a Canadian airport. An eTA is quick to apply for online and is usually valid for up to five years or until your passport expires.

Option 1: International Experience Canada (IEC)

IEC is often the easiest way for young Brits to start working in Canada.

The Working Holiday category provides an open work permit, letting you work for almost any employer. Canadian work experience gained on IEC can later support permanent residence through the Canadian Experience Class.

Option 2: Employer-Supported Work Permits

If you are older than 35 or want a specific job, a Canadian employer may support an LMIA-based work permit or use an LMIA exemption. UK professionals may also qualify for intra-company transfers or categories under the Canada-UK trade agreement.

Option 3: Express Entry

UK applicants often score strongly in Express Entry because of native-level English, degrees and professional experience. Federal Skilled Worker candidates still need a language test, even as native speakers, plus an Educational Credential Assessment for UK qualifications and proof of funds. Brits who learn French can benefit from French category draws.

Option 4: Provincial Nominee Programs

Provinces such as Alberta, British Columbia and Saskatchewan recruit health care workers, tradespeople and professionals. Many UK nurses, electricians and engineers explore provincial streams, often with a Canadian job offer.

Option 5: Family Sponsorship

If your spouse or partner is Canadian or a permanent resident, they may be able to sponsor you.

Practical Considerations for Brits

  • Credential recognition: regulated professions such as nursing, teaching and engineering require provincial licensing.
  • Driving: rules for exchanging UK licences vary by province.
  • Health care: provincial coverage may have waiting periods, so arrange private insurance for your first months.
  • Pensions and taxes: get advice on UK pensions and Canadian tax residency.
  • Police certificates: you may need an ACRO police certificate for permanent residence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I move to Canada from the UK without a job offer?

Yes. Young Brits aged 18 to 35 can apply for International Experience Canada, including the Working Holiday category, which does not require a job offer. Skilled workers can also apply for permanent residence through Express Entry without a job offer if they qualify and rank high enough.

Do UK citizens need a visa for Canada?

No. UK citizens are visa-exempt but need an Electronic Travel Authorization to fly to or transit through a Canadian airport. To work or study in Canada, UK citizens need the appropriate permit, such as an IEC work permit or a study permit.

What is the age limit for IEC for UK citizens?

For the 2026 season, UK citizens aged 18 to 35 can apply for International Experience Canada. UK participants can receive permits of up to two years and can participate up to two times, across the Working Holiday, Young Professionals and International Co-op categories.

Do native English speakers need a language test for Express Entry?

Yes. All Express Entry candidates must take an approved language test, even if English is their first language. UK applicants usually take IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General or PTE Core. High scores add significant Comprehensive Ranking System points.
