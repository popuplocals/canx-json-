Express Entry favors younger applicants, with points allocated based on age. The ideal age range is 20-29 years old, receiving the maximum 100 points. Points decrease gradually for applicants aged 30 and above, with no points awarded after age 45.

Language proficiency

Proficiency in English and/or French is crucial. Applicants must take approved language tests like IELTS or CELPIP for English, and TEF for French. The Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) scale is used to assess language skills:

      • Reading
      • Writing
      • Listening
      • Speaking

A minimum CLB 7 is required for most Express Entry programs, with higher scores earning more points.

Education Qualifications

Educational credentials are evaluated based on Canadian standards. Points are awarded for:

      • Secondary school (high school) diploma
      • Post-secondary certificate or diploma
      • Bachelor’s degree
      • Master’s degree or Doctorate

Foreign credentials must be assessed through an Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) to determine Canadian equivalency.

Work Experience

Skilled work experience is a key factor in Express Entry eligibility. Requirements include:

      • At least one year of continuous full-time work (or equivalent part-time)
      • In a skilled occupation (TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3)
      • Within the last 10 years

More years of relevant experience can increase your CRS score.

Proof of funds

Unless you have a valid job offer or are already working in Canada, you must show proof of sufficient funds to support yourself and your family upon arrival. The amount required depends on family size and is updated annually.
