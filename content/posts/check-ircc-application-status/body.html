You submitted your application, paid the fees and uploaded every document. Now you refresh your account every morning, hoping for a change. If that sounds familiar, you are not alone. Learning how to check your IRCC application status properly will not make IRCC move faster, but it will help you spot requests early, understand what each update means, and know when it is time to act.

This guide explains every official tool Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) offers in 2026, which one fits your application, how to read common status messages, and what to do when nothing seems to change.

Quick Summary

You can check most IRCC applications online once you receive an acknowledgement of receipt. The Client Application Status tool covers Express Entry, family sponsorship, PR cards, citizenship certificates and more. Your IRCC secure account, the Permanent Residence Portal and the citizenship tracker show updates and messages for the applications submitted there. Always check your account and email regularly, because missing an IRCC request can lead to refusal.

Before You Start: The Acknowledgement of Receipt

You cannot check status until IRCC confirms it has received a complete application. This confirmation is called an Acknowledgement of Receipt (AOR). It may arrive by email, in your online account, or by mail for paper applications. Your AOR includes the numbers you need to check status, such as your application number and your Unique Client Identifier (UCI).

If you have not received an AOR, IRCC may still be checking your application for completeness. Paper applications usually take longer to reach this stage than online applications.

IRCC uses several tools, and the right one depends on how and what you applied for. You can find all of them on IRCC’s check your application status page.

Step by Step: Checking Your Status

Step 1: Gather your numbers

Locate your AOR and note your application number, UCI and the exact name and date of birth you used on your forms. Small differences, such as a missing middle name, can stop the tool from finding your file.

Step 2: Sign in or use the right tool

If you applied online, sign in to the account you used to apply. If you applied on paper, use the Client Application Status tool, or link your paper application to your IRCC secure account so you can see updates in one place.

Step 3: Review every section, not just the headline

Look at the details for each stage: eligibility, background check, medical exam and biometrics. Check the messages section too. IRCC often places important letters, such as requests for documents, inside your account rather than sending them by mail.

Step 4: Check your email, including spam

IRCC sends many notifications by email. Add IRCC addresses to your safe senders list and check your spam folder every few days.

Status labels differ between tools and programs, so focus on what the message asks you to do rather than the exact wording.

How Often Does IRCC Update Status?

IRCC updates its tools as officers act on files, and the Client Application Status tool refreshes daily. Long periods without visible change are common, especially during background checks. A quiet account usually means your file is waiting in a queue, not that something has gone wrong.

When Your Status Has Not Changed for Months

If your file has passed the posted processing time on the official IRCC processing times tool, you have several options:

  • Submit a case-specific enquiry through IRCC’s official web form.
  • Call the IRCC Client Support Centre at 1-888-242-2100 (Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. local time, within Canada).
  • Request your Global Case Management System (GCMS) notes to see officer entries and pending steps.
  • Ask a regulated consultant to review your file for gaps or outstanding requests.

IRCC’s call centre agents generally see the same information you see online, so check your account thoroughly before calling.

Mistakes That Cost Applicants Time

  • Missing an IRCC request because it sat unread in the account or spam folder.
  • Using a different email address from the one on your application.
  • Moving without updating your address with IRCC.
  • Submitting duplicate applications in an attempt to speed things up.
  • Creating multiple accounts and losing track of where the application lives.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check my IRCC application status online?

Use the tool that matches your application. Sign in to the account you applied through, such as your IRCC secure account or the Permanent Residence Portal, or use the Client Application Status tool for Express Entry, family sponsorship, PR cards and several other applications. You need the details from your acknowledgement of receipt.

Why can't I see my application status?

You usually cannot check status until IRCC sends an acknowledgement of receipt confirming your application is complete. Other common causes include entering details that do not exactly match your forms, using the wrong tool for your application type, or not linking a paper application to your online account.

What does "background check in progress" mean?

It means IRCC and its partner agencies are completing security and criminality checks. This stage is routine for permanent residence and many temporary applications. It can take weeks or months, and IRCC rarely explains the length. A long background check does not mean your application will be refused.

How often does the IRCC status tracker update?

IRCC updates status as officers act on your file, and the Client Application Status tool refreshes daily. Many files show no visible change for long periods, especially during background checks. Check weekly and read every message carefully rather than refreshing several times a day.
