What Is Family Sponsorship in Canada?

Family sponsorship is a key Canadian immigration pathway that allows Canadian citizens and permanent residents to sponsor certain family members for permanent residence. This includes:

    • Spouses or common-law partners
    • Dependent children
    • Parents and grandparents

This program helps keep families together and supports the emotional and economic integration of newcomers.

Why Are Quebec Processing Times Longer?

Quebec has its own immigration agreement with the federal government. This means:

    • Additional steps are required through Quebec’s Ministry of Immigration (MIFI).
    • There is a dual approval process: first by IRCC, then by the Quebec government.
    • This extra layer typically leads to longer processing times.

Outside Quebec, the process is more streamlined because IRCC handles the entire assessment.

Tips to Speed Up (or at Least Not Delay) Your Sponsorship Application

While there’s no way to fast-track a family sponsorship application, avoiding errors can save you months:

    1. Submit a complete application: Use IRCC’s checklist and double-check all forms, signatures, and documents.
    2. Provide strong relationship evidence: Photos, communication logs, travel records, joint accounts, etc.
    3. Translate documents properly: All non-English/French documents must include certified translations.
    4. Watch for IRCC emails: Respond promptly if they request more info.
    5. Stay organized: Track deadlines, retain copies, and document communication.

FAQs About Family Sponsorship Processing

How often does IRCC update processing times?
Monthly. Check IRCC’s website for current timelines.

Why is Quebec slower?
Because Quebec applicants must go through both IRCC and MIFI (Quebec’s provincial immigration ministry), adding extra steps and time.

Can I do anything to speed up my case?
No expedited processing is available, but submitting a well-prepared and complete application avoids unnecessary delays.

What happens if there’s a delay?
If you’ve submitted everything and there’s no IRCC request pending, your file may simply be in the queue. You can submit a webform to request an update.

Who can I sponsor?
Spouses, common-law or conjugal partners, dependent children, parents, and grandparents.
