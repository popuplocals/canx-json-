Visiting Canada From the Philippines

Filipino citizens normally need a visitor visa. However, the Philippines is one of the countries whose citizens can apply for an Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) instead of a visa if they have held a Canadian visitor visa in the past 10 years or currently hold a valid United States non-immigrant visa, and are flying to or through a Canadian airport.

Pathway 1: Express Entry

Filipino nurses, engineers, IT professionals and other skilled workers can qualify through the Federal Skilled Worker Program or, after working in Canada, the Canadian Experience Class. In 2026, category-based draws for healthcare and social services and for trades offered lower cut-offs than general Canadian Experience Class draws. Strong English scores make a major difference.

Pathway 2: Provincial Nominee Programs

Provinces such as Alberta, British Columbia, Saskatchewan and Manitoba have long recruited Filipino workers in health care, trades, agriculture and other sectors. In 2026, several provinces prioritize health care and skilled trades. Many streams require a job offer or experience in the province.

Pathway 3: Employer-Supported Work Permits

Many Filipinos come to Canada with an employer-specific work permit supported by a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA). Canadian work experience can later support permanent residence through the Canadian Experience Class or a provincial program. Remember that genuine employers never charge workers for jobs or LMIAs.

Pathway 4: Caregiving

The Home Care Worker Immigration Pilots offered a direct route to permanent residence for home child care providers and home support workers. IRCC paused new applications from March 31, 2026 to March 30, 2027. Caregivers already in Canada should keep their status valid and explore other options, such as provincial programs or the Canadian Experience Class, if eligible.

Pathway 5: Study and Family

Filipino students can study in Canada and later pursue a Post-Graduation Work Permit and permanent residence. Canadian citizens and permanent residents can sponsor spouses, partners and dependent children from the Philippines.

Beware of Recruitment Scams

Fake job offers and illegal recruitment fees are common risks. Protect yourself:

  • Never pay for a job offer or an LMIA.
  • Verify employers and recruiters before sharing documents.
  • Work only with licensed Canadian immigration consultants or lawyers.
  • Be cautious of offers that promise guaranteed visas or fast permanent residence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Filipinos need a visa to visit Canada?

Usually, yes. However, Filipino citizens who have held a Canadian visitor visa in the past 10 years or currently hold a valid US non-immigrant visa may apply for an Electronic Travel Authorization instead when flying to or through a Canadian airport. Everyone else needs a visitor visa.

What is the best pathway from the Philippines to Canada in 2026?

It depends on your profile. Skilled workers with strong English may qualify through Express Entry, especially in healthcare or trades categories. Others may use employer-supported work permits or provincial programs. The caregiver pilots are paused for new applications until March 30, 2027, so caregivers should explore other options.

Are the caregiver pilots open in 2026?

No. IRCC paused new applications to the Home Care Worker Immigration Pilots from March 31, 2026 to March 30, 2027. Caregivers already in Canada should maintain valid status and explore alternatives such as the Canadian Experience Class or provincial programs if they qualify.

What police certificate do Filipinos need for Canadian immigration?

Filipino applicants generally need an NBI clearance from the National Bureau of Investigation, plus certificates from any other country where they lived for six months or more since age 18. Follow IRCC’s country-specific instructions and timing rules, and submit colour scans of the originals.
