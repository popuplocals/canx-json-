A Post-Graduation Work Permit can be the most valuable document an international student ever receives. It lets you work for almost any employer in Canada and build the Canadian experience that powers most permanent residence applications. But you get only one in your lifetime, and since November 1, 2024, eligibility depends on language test results and, for some programs, your field of study.

This guide explains PGWP rules in 2026: who qualifies, how long your permit will last, the language and field of study requirements, how to apply within the 180-day window, and how to turn your PGWP into permanent residence.

Quick Summary

To qualify for a PGWP in 2026, you generally need to complete an eligible program of at least 8 months at a PGWP-eligible institution, apply within 180 days of confirmation of completion, and meet language requirements: CLB 7 for university graduates or CLB 5 for college graduates. Graduates of non-degree programs who applied for their first study permit on or after November 1, 2024 must also study in an eligible field. Master’s graduates can receive up to three years. You can only get one PGWP in your lifetime.

Who Is Eligible for a PGWP?

According to IRCC’s PGWP eligibility rules, you generally must:

  • Complete a program of at least 8 months at a PGWP-eligible designated learning institution.
  • Maintain full-time student status during each academic session, except permitted breaks and the final session.
  • Apply within 180 days of receiving confirmation that you completed your program.
  • Have held a valid study permit at some point during that 180-day window.
  • Meet the language requirement for your level of study.
  • Meet the field of study requirement, if it applies to your program.

You are not eligible if you already received a PGWP, if you studied only English or French as a second language, or if more than 50% of your program was completed through distance learning, subject to limited exceptions.

Language Requirements

Use an approved test, such as IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General, PTE Core, TEF Canada or TCF Canada, and make sure results are less than two years old when you apply.

Field of Study Requirement

Graduates of bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral degree programs do not need to meet a field of study requirement. Graduates of other programs, such as college diplomas and certificates, whose first study permit application was submitted on or after November 1, 2024, must graduate from a program linked to an occupation in long-term shortage, in categories such as agriculture and agri-food, health care, science, technology, engineering and mathematics (STEM), trades and transport. IRCC publishes the list of eligible programs by Classification of Instructional Programs (CIP) code. Check your program code before you enrol, not after you graduate.

Your PGWP cannot extend beyond your passport’s expiry date, so renew your passport before you apply if needed.

How to Apply for Your PGWP: Step by Step

Step 1: Get your completion documents

Obtain your final transcript and an official letter confirming you completed your program. Your 180 days start from the date you receive this confirmation.

Step 2: Take your language test early

Book your language test well before graduation so results are ready when you apply.

Step 3: Confirm your field of study, if required

Match your program’s CIP code against IRCC’s eligible list.

Step 4: Apply online before your study permit expires

If you apply from inside Canada before your study permit expires and meet the conditions, you may be able to work full-time while IRCC processes your application. Apply online; flagpoling for PGWPs ended on December 23, 2024.

Step 5: Track your application and respond quickly

Watch your IRCC account for requests, including biometrics.

From PGWP to Permanent Residence

Most PGWP holders aim for the Canadian Experience Class, which requires at least one year of skilled Canadian work experience in TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3 occupations. Others pursue provincial nominee streams. Plan your PR path from day one, because your PGWP cannot be extended in most cases.

  • Work in a skilled occupation as early as possible.
  • Keep pay stubs, contracts and reference letters for every job.
  • Improve your language scores to raise your Express Entry score.
  • Track provincial programs, many of which changed significantly in 2026.

Common PGWP Mistakes

  • Missing the 180-day deadline.
  • Applying without valid language test results.
  • Enrolling in a non-degree program outside the eligible fields.
  • Letting your passport expire, which shortens your permit.
  • Taking more than half of your program online.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the PGWP requirements in 2026?

You generally need to complete an eligible program of at least 8 months at a PGWP-eligible institution, apply within 180 days of completion, have held a valid study permit during that window, meet CLB 7 for university degrees or CLB 5 for college programs, and meet the field of study requirement if it applies.

Do all PGWP applicants need to meet the field of study requirement?

No. Bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral degree graduates are exempt. Graduates of other programs, such as college diplomas and certificates, generally must meet the field of study requirement if their first study permit application was submitted on or after November 1, 2024. Check IRCC’s eligible program list using your program’s CIP code.

How long is a PGWP for a master's degree?

Graduates of eligible master’s degree programs of at least 8 months can generally receive a PGWP of up to three years, even if the program itself was shorter than two years. Your permit cannot extend beyond your passport’s expiry date, so check your passport validity before applying.

Can I work while my PGWP application is processing?

If you apply from inside Canada before your study permit expires and you meet the conditions, you may be able to work full-time while IRCC processes your PGWP application. Leaving Canada can affect this. Keep proof of your application submission date and your completion letter.
