Studying in Canada opens doors to world-class education and career opportunities, but international students must ensure their study permit remains valid throughout their academic journey. If your study permit is set to expire and you plan to continue your studies, applying for a study permit extension is crucial to maintain your legal status and avoid disruptions.

Here’s a complete guide for students looking to extend their study permit in Canada in 2025, with accurate information and insights tailored to the current IRCC regulations.

Why Extending Your Study Permit Is Important

Canada requires all international students to have a valid study permit for the full duration of their studies. If your permit expires before you complete your program, you must apply for an extension. Failing to do so may result in the loss of your student status and even require you to leave the country.

When to Apply for a Study Permit Extension

    • Apply at least 90 days before your current study permit expires.
    • It is strongly recommended to apply 4 to 6 months in advance, especially considering current IRCC processing times.
    • Your study permit cannot be extended past the expiry of your passport, so renew your passport first if needed.

Who Can Apply

You must meet the following criteria to apply for a study permit extension:

    • Be physically present in Canada at the time of application.
    • Hold a valid study permit or be within 90 days of expiration (if restoring status).
    • Be actively enrolled and making progress at a Designated Learning Institution (DLI).
    • Plan to stay enrolled in the same or similar academic program during the extension period.

If you change your institution or study level, additional documentation may be required, such as a new Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL).

What Is Maintained Status?

If you apply to extend your study permit before it expires, you will benefit from maintained status. This means you can legally continue studying in Canada under the same conditions as your expired permit until a decision is made on your application.

However, if your current permit expires before you apply, you will lose your student status and must apply for restoration of status within 90 days. During this period, you are not allowed to study or work in Canada.

Step-by-Step Process to Extend Your Study Permit

Step 1: Gather All Required Documents

Before starting your application, make sure you have the following documents ready:

    • IMM5709 Form: “Application to Change Conditions, Extend My Stay or Remain in Canada as a Student.”
    • Letter of Enrollment: A recent letter from your current institution confirming your active enrollment.
    • Academic Transcript: Optional but recommended, especially if requested by IRCC or your institution.
    • Valid Passport: Must be valid for the full period you are requesting.
    • Current Study Permit: A scanned copy of your existing permit.
    • Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL): If applicable. This is sometimes required when there are changes to your study level or institution.
    • Recent Passport-Size Photo.
    • Proof of Financial Support: This could include bank statements, proof of tuition fee payment, scholarship or funding letters. Ensure you demonstrate that you can cover both tuition and living expenses for the extended period.
    • Other Supporting Documents: Any documents listed in your IRCC document checklist generated during the online application process.

If you are studying in Quebec, you must also include a valid Quebec Approval Certificate (CAQ) for the duration of your new study permit.

Step 2: Sign In to Your IRCC Secure Account

Create an account or log in to your existing IRCC Secure Account. This is where you will complete your application, pay the fees, upload documents, and check your application status. Do not use the IRCC Portal, as it does not support study permit extension applications.

Step 3: Complete the Online Questionnaire

Answer the prompts carefully. This determines your eligibility and generates a personalized checklist of required documents and forms based on your situation.

Step 4: Upload Documents and Fill Out Forms

Fill in the IMM5709 form and upload all supporting documents mentioned in your personalized checklist. Ensure all documents are legible and up-to-date.

Step 5: Pay the Required Fees

    • The study permit extension application fee is $150 CAD.
    • If biometrics are required, an additional $85 CAD must be paid.
    • All payments are made online through your IRCC Secure Account.

Step 6: Submit Your Application

Review your application thoroughly before submission. Once submitted successfully, you’ll receive an Acknowledgement of Receipt (AOR) confirming your application is under review. Keep a copy of this confirmation and all filed documents for your records.

After You Apply: What Happens Next?

    • Monitoring your application: You can log in to your IRCC secure account to track the status.
    • Decision timelines: Processing times can vary from a few weeks to several months, depending on your country of origin, volume of applications, and your individual case.
    • Biometric instructions: If biometrics are requested, attend the nearest Service Canada location or biometric collection center promptly.

If you applied before your current permit expired, you may continue studying under maintained status until a decision is made.

If Your Study Permit Has Expired

If your study permit has expired and you did not apply for an extension in time, you may apply to restore your student status within 90 days of the permit expiry. You will need to pay:

    • Restoration of Status Fee: $239.75 CAD
    • New Study Permit Fee: $150 CAD
      Total Payment: $389.75 CAD
      Check the official website for the latest fee details. 

During this time, you are not allowed to study or work until IRCC approves your restoration and study permit extension.

Common Questions About Study Permit Extensions

Can I extend my study permit while outside Canada?
No. You must be inside Canada to apply for a study permit extension. If you are abroad, you will need to apply for a new study permit from outside Canada.

Is a Provincial Attestation Letter mandatory?
Not in all cases. If you’re continuing at the same institution and study level, you may be exempt. However, always verify this with the most recent IRCC guidelines or consult with your school.

Can I travel while my extension is being processed?
It is best to avoid travel. Leaving Canada while your extension is pending could impact your maintained status or re-entry if you don’t have valid documents.

Final Tips for a Smooth Application Process

    • Start early to avoid last-minute complications or loss of status.
    • Keep copies of everything you submit.
    • Double-check names, dates, and document formats.
    • Ensure your passport is valid for the full extension period you are requesting.
    • Consult with your school’s international student office or a licensed immigration consultant if you feel uncertain about any step.
