When it comes to bringing your spouse or common-law partner to Canada as a permanent resident, one of the first and most important decisions you’ll face is: Should you apply from inside or outside Canada?

While both inland and outland spousal sponsorships aim to reunite families, they differ significantly in terms of processing time, eligibility, work permit access, and more.

This blog breaks down the two options in 2025, helping you choose the best route based on your situation.

What Is Spousal Sponsorship?

Spousal sponsorship is a Canadian immigration program designed to reunite families. Canadian citizens and permanent residents can sponsor their spouse, common-law, or conjugal partner for permanent residency through two main options:

    • Inland Sponsorship – for couples living together in Canada
    • Outland Sponsorship – for couples where one partner lives outside Canada or cannot stay together during processing

Each option has unique benefits and potential challenges. Knowing these can help you pick the most suitable route for you and your loved one.

Inland Spousal Sponsorship

Who is it for?

Ideal if your spouse or partner is already in Canada with valid temporary status and you want to stay together while the application is being processed.

Benefits:

    • Stay together in Canada throughout the process
    • Apply for an open work permit to allow your spouse to work legally while waiting
    • No need to return home during the application

Considerations:

    • Travel is limited: Leaving Canada during processing may cancel your application
    • No appeal rights: If refused, judicial review is the only recourse
    • Longer processing times: Sometimes takes up to 28 months

If being together in Canada during the entire sponsorship process is your priority, Inland sponsorship is worth considering.

Outland Spousal Sponsorship

Who is it for?

Suitable for couples where the spouse or partner lives outside Canada, or when flexibility to travel during processing is needed.

Benefits:

    • Flexible travel: Your spouse can live abroad or visit Canada during processing (visitor visa permitting)
    • Faster processing times: Typically between 8 to 16 months
    • Right to appeal: Refusals can be appealed to the Immigration Appeal Division
    • No requirement to stay physically in Canada during processing

Considerations:

    • Work permits are limited to those already in Canada with valid status
    • Possibility of separation during the process if visits aren’t feasible

Outland sponsorship is a good option when flexibility and appeal rights are important.

Important Updates for 2025

    • Online applications only: All spousal sponsorships must now be submitted digitally, so accuracy and completeness are critical.
    • More scrutiny: Applications are reviewed carefully to prevent fraud because thorough and genuine documentation is essential.
    • Open work permit availability: Inland applicants continue to benefit from the spousal open work permit, helping couples financially.
    • Processing times fluctuate: Times vary by country and case type; Outland is often faster, but not always.

Common Questions

Which option is faster?
Outland sponsorship generally has faster processing times, but this can vary depending on individual cases and countries.

Can my spouse work while waiting?
Yes. Inland applicants can apply for a spousal open work permit. Outland applicants may work only if they already hold a valid work or study permit.

Can we travel during the application?
With Inland sponsorship, travel outside Canada during processing can jeopardize the application. Outland applicants have more freedom to travel internationally.

What if the application is refused?
Inland applicants cannot appeal refusals but may request judicial review. Outland applicants have a formal right to appeal.

Which Path Is Right for You?

Consider your living situation, travel needs, work permit requirements, and whether having appeal rights is important to you. The best choice aligns with your personal and family circumstances.
