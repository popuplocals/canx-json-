When applying for permanent residence in Canada on Humanitarian and Compassionate Grounds Letter Sample (H&C), the most important part of your application is the submission letter. This is your opportunity to explain your unique situation and ask Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) to consider an exemption from the usual immigration rules.

Because H&C applications are discretionary and based on exceptional circumstances, your letter must be personal, detailed, and supported by evidence. In this blog, we explain how to write a strong Humanitarian and Compassionate Grounds Letter Sample, what elements to include, and how to present your case effectively.

What Is an Humanitarian and Compassionate Grounds Letter Sample?

An Humanitarian and Compassionate Grounds Letter Sample is a written statement that forms the core of your application under section 25(1) of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act. It is your chance to ask IRCC to grant you permanent residence or another immigration relief by considering:

    • Hardship you would face if forced to leave Canada
    • Establishment and integration in Canada
    • The best interests of any children affected
    • Any other compelling or compassionate factors

The letter is usually written by the applicant or by a legal representative on their behalf, and must include all key facts, supporting documents, and legal arguments in a clear and respectful manner.

Who Can Apply Under H&C Grounds?

Humanitarian and Compassionate Grounds Letter Sample are typically used by:

    • Individuals without legal status in Canada
    • Failed refugee claimants with ties to Canada
    • Long-term residents who do not meet other immigration criteria
    • Parents of Canadian citizen or permanent resident children
    • People with medical, personal, or family-related hardships

Each case is assessed on its own merits. There is no formal checklist, which is why the quality of your submission letter is crucial.

Key Components of a Strong Humanitarian and Compassionate Grounds Letter Sample

To strengthen your application, your letter should be structured, well-reasoned, and supported by evidence. Below are the core components every Strong Humanitarian and Compassionate Grounds Letter Sample should contain:

1. Introduction and Purpose

    • Clearly state that you are applying for consideration under section 25(1)
    • Include your full name, UCI number (if applicable), and immigration history
    • Briefly summarize the request (e.g., applying for permanent residence based on H&C grounds)

Example:
 “I respectfully request that IRCC consider my application for permanent residence on Humanitarian and Compassionate Grounds Letter Sample under section 25(1) of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act.”

2. Personal Background and Immigration History

    • Describe your life before coming to Canada
    • Explain your entry into Canada and immigration timeline
    • Mention any previous applications, refusals, or interactions with IRCC or CBSA

3. Establishment in Canada

    • Show how long you have lived in Canada
    • Detail your employment history, community involvement, education, and social integration
    • Mention any volunteer work, taxes paid, and support from Canadian residents or organizations
    • Include letters of reference or support from employers, community members, or religious leaders

4. Hardship if Removed from Canada

    • Explain why returning to your country of origin would cause serious hardship
    • Include risks related to safety, health care, financial instability, lack of support, or discrimination
    • Provide country-specific evidence where applicable (e.g., country reports, news articles, medical documents)

5. Best Interests of the Child (if applicable)

    • Describe the impact on any Canadian-born or resident children
    • Explain their schooling, health, emotional well-being, and support networks in Canada
    • Include school records, doctor’s letters, psychological assessments, or custody documents

IRCC gives significant weight to the best interests of affected children, so this section must be well developed.

6. Medical or Compassionate Grounds

    • Explain any medical conditions (your own or family members)
    • Provide proof of diagnosis, treatment plans, and whether appropriate care is available in your home country
    • Describe emotional or psychological trauma related to removal

7. Supporting Documents

    • Reference each attachment clearly in your letter
    • Include only relevant and credible documents such as:
      • Medical records
      • School or daycare letters
      • Letters of support
      • Proof of residency or work
      • Legal documents (e.g., restraining orders, custody agreements)
      • Country condition reports

8. Conclusion and Request

    • Restate your request for Humanitarian and Compassionate Grounds Letter Sample relief
    • Express your respect for Canada’s immigration system and your sincere hope for a fair decision
    • Thank the officer for their time and consideration

Example:
 “I respectfully ask that you consider my personal circumstances, my strong ties to Canada, and the best interests of my children when reviewing this application. I remain committed to contributing positively to Canadian society.”

Additional Tips for Writing

    • Keep your tone respectful and honest
    • Avoid exaggeration or emotional manipulation
    • Use clear, simple language
    • Organize your points logically
    • Number or label your attachments for easy reference
    • Consider having a legal professional review or draft the letter if possible
