A.  Identity documents

When applying for Express Entry, providing accurate and valid identity documents is crucial. These documents serve as proof of your identity and personal information. Here’s a list of essential identity documents you’ll need to submit:

      • Valid passport or travel document
      • Birth certificate
      • Marriage certificate or divorce papers (if applicable)
      • National identity card (if available in your country)

It’s important to ensure that all documents are current and not expired. If any document is not in English or French, you must provide a certified translation along with the original.

B.  Language test results

Language proficiency is a key factor in Express Entry eligibility. You must provide official language test results from an approved testing organization. The two main tests accepted are:

Test

English

French

Results must be less than 2 years old at the time of application

  

Your test results should demonstrate proficiency in reading, writing, listening, and speaking. The minimum language requirements vary depending on the specific Express Entry program you’re applying for.

C.  Educational credentials

To support your Express Entry application, you’ll need to provide proof of your educational qualifications. This includes:

      • Diplomas, degrees, and certificates
      • Transcripts showing courses taken and grades achieved

If you obtained your education outside Canada, you’ll need to get an Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) to verify that your foreign education is equivalent to a Canadian qualification.

D.  Work experience

Documenting your work experience is crucial for Express Entry. You should gather the following:

      • Detailed reference letters from previous employers
      • Employment contracts
      • Pay stubs or tax documents

These documents should clearly outline your job title, duties, dates of employment, and hours worked per week. Ensure that your work experience aligns with the National Occupational Classification (NOC) system used by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
