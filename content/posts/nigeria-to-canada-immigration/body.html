Quick Summary

Nigerian citizens need a visitor visa to travel to Canada. The main pathways to permanent residence are Express Entry, provincial nominee programs, study-to-PR and family sponsorship. Strong English scores, a credible Educational Credential Assessment, detailed reference letters, well-documented proof of funds and a police certificate are essential. In 2026, category-based Express Entry draws, especially for healthcare, offer important opportunities.

Why Express Entry Works Well for Many Nigerians

Many Nigerian applicants hold university degrees, professional experience and strong English skills, all of which score well in the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS). Federal Skilled Worker candidates need at least one year of continuous skilled work experience, CLB 7 language ability, an Educational Credential Assessment for foreign education, proof of settlement funds and enough points on the 67-point eligibility grid.

Step 1: Assess Your Eligibility and Score

Calculate your CRS score honestly. Consider your age, education, language results, work experience, and your spouse’s profile. Identify whether you qualify for category-based draws, such as healthcare and social services, which carried lower cut-offs than general draws in 2026.

Step 2: Take Your Language Test

Take IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General or PTE Core. Aim for CLB 9 or higher in all four abilities, which unlocks major transferability points. Many Nigerian candidates gain more points from a language retest than from any other single change.

Step 3: Get Your Educational Credential Assessment

Order an ECA from an IRCC-designated organization. Start early, because universities must often send transcripts directly, which can take time.

Step 4: Prepare Strong Reference Letters

Reference letters should be on company letterhead and include your job title, duties, hours per week, salary, start and end dates, and the signer’s contact information. Duties should align with your chosen National Occupational Classification (NOC) code. Officers may verify employment, so accuracy matters.

Step 5: Document Your Proof of Funds Properly

Federal Skilled Worker applicants must show settlement funds, unless exempt. IRCC updates the required amounts annually. Officers look for:

  • Funds held in your name, or jointly with your spouse.
  • A consistent history of savings, usually covering several months.
  • Official bank letters with account details and balances.
  • Explanations and evidence for large deposits, such as property sales or gifts.

Borrowed funds and sudden unexplained deposits are common reasons for concern.

Step 6: Get Your Police Certificate

Obtain a police certificate from the Nigeria Police Force following IRCC’s country-specific instructions, plus certificates from any other country where you lived for six months or more since age 18. Start early, because processing can take time.

Step 7: Enter the Pool and Explore Provinces

Create your Express Entry profile, select provinces of interest and register in provincial systems where you qualify. A provincial nomination adds 600 CRS points.

Other Pathways

  • Study in Canada: master’s and doctoral students at public institutions no longer need a provincial attestation letter since January 1, 2026, and graduates can pursue PGWPs and permanent residence.
  • Work permits: employer-supported permits can lead to Canadian experience and PR.
  • Family sponsorship: citizens and permanent residents can sponsor spouses, partners and dependent children.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I move from Nigeria to Canada in 2026?

Most Nigerian professionals use Express Entry, especially the Federal Skilled Worker Program, supported by strong English scores, an ECA and detailed reference letters. Provincial nominee programs, study-to-PR and family sponsorship are other options. Category-based draws, especially for healthcare, can offer lower cut-offs than general draws.

Do Nigerians need a visa to visit Canada?

Yes. Nigerian citizens need a visitor visa (temporary resident visa) to travel to Canada and must give biometrics. Applicants should show a clear purpose of travel, strong ties to Nigeria and sufficient funds. A visitor visa does not authorize work or long-term study in Canada.

How much proof of funds do I need for Express Entry from Nigeria?

Federal Skilled Worker and Federal Skilled Trades applicants must show settlement funds based on family size, unless exempt. IRCC updates the required amounts every year, so check the current table. Officers expect funds in your name with a consistent savings history and explanations for large deposits.

How do I get a police certificate from Nigeria for Canada?

Nigerian applicants obtain a police certificate from the Nigeria Police Force, following IRCC’s country-specific instructions. You also need certificates from any other country where you lived for six months or more since age 18. Start early, because processing times can be long.
