The United Arab Emirates is home to millions of skilled expatriate professionals, many of whom see Canada as their long-term destination. Engineers, finance professionals, nurses, IT specialists and managers working in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and across the Emirates often have exactly the education, experience and English skills that Canada’s programs reward. But immigrating from the UAE has its own quirks, from nationality-based visa rules to police certificates from more than one country.

This guide explains how UAE residents can immigrate to Canada in 2026: pathways, visa rules, key documents and practical tips for expatriates.

Quick Summary

Your nationality, not your UAE residence, decides whether you need a Canadian visitor visa. UAE citizens are visa-exempt and need an Electronic Travel Authorization. Expatriates follow the rules for their citizenship. Skilled UAE residents often qualify through Express Entry or provincial nominee programs. Expect to provide police certificates from the UAE and from every other country where you lived for six months or more since age 18.

Visa Rules: Nationality Comes First

Canada applies visa rules based on your citizenship. UAE citizens are visa-exempt and need an Electronic Travel Authorization (eTA) to fly to Canada. Expatriates living in the UAE need a visitor visa or an eTA based on their own nationality. For example, Indian, Pakistani and Nigerian citizens living in the UAE still need visitor visas.

Pathway 1: Express Entry

UAE residents often score well in Express Entry thanks to strong education, professional experience and English skills. Most apply through the Federal Skilled Worker Program, which requires at least one year of continuous skilled work experience, CLB 7 language, an Educational Credential Assessment, proof of funds and at least 67 points on the eligibility grid.

Foreign work experience counts

Skilled work experience gained in the UAE counts as foreign work experience. Combined with strong language scores, three or more years of foreign experience can earn up to 50 skill transferability points.

Pathway 2: Provincial Nominee Programs

Some provinces select candidates from the Express Entry pool or through their own systems. A nomination adds 600 CRS points. Many streams favour candidates with job offers or connections to the province, so research each province’s 2026 priorities.

Pathway 3: Study, Work and Family

  • Study: UAE residents can apply for study permits from the UAE; graduate students at public institutions are exempt from provincial attestation letters since January 1, 2026.
  • Work: multinational employers may transfer staff to Canada through intra-company transfers.
  • Family: Canadian citizens and permanent residents can sponsor spouses, partners and dependent children living in the UAE.

Practical Tips for Expatriates

  • Start police certificate requests early, especially for your home country.
  • Keep end-of-service and salary documents organized as employment proof.
  • Plan for the transition from tax-free UAE income to Canadian taxation.
  • Consider timing your move around children’s school years.
  • Keep your UAE residence valid until your Canadian plans are confirmed.

Common Mistakes UAE Residents Make

  • Assuming UAE residence changes their visa requirements.
  • Forgetting police certificates from past countries of residence.
  • Submitting reference letters that lack required detail.
  • Using unlicensed agents or paying for job offers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can UAE residents apply for Canadian immigration from the UAE?

Yes. UAE residents can apply for Express Entry, provincial nominee programs, study permits and other pathways while living in the UAE. Your eligibility depends on your profile, not your residence. Your nationality determines whether you need a visitor visa or an Electronic Travel Authorization to travel.

Do UAE citizens need a visa for Canada?

No. UAE citizens are visa-exempt but need an Electronic Travel Authorization to fly to or transit through a Canadian airport. Expatriates living in the UAE follow the visa rules for their own citizenship, so many still need a Canadian visitor visa.

Which police certificates do UAE residents need for Canada PR?

You generally need a police certificate from the UAE and from every other country, including your home country, where you lived for six months or more in a row since age 18. Follow IRCC’s country-specific instructions and timing rules for your current and past countries of residence.

Does UAE work experience count for Express Entry?

Yes. Skilled work experience in the UAE counts as foreign work experience for Express Entry. It can help you meet Federal Skilled Worker requirements and, combined with strong language scores, earn up to 50 skill transferability points for three or more years of experience.
