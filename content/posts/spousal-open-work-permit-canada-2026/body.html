Waiting 15 to 24 months for a permanent residence decision is manageable when both partners are working and financially stable. It becomes significantly harder when the sponsored person cannot work at all during that period. The Spousal Open Work Permit (SOWP) exists to solve this problem.

The SOWP allows the sponsored spouse or partner to work for any employer in Canada while the permanent residence application is being processed. No Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) is required. No specific job offer is needed. No employer has to be named in advance. The permit is open, meaning the holder can take any legal employment in Canada, change jobs, work part-time or full-time, and switch industries, all without returning to IRCC for a new permit.

This guide explains exactly who qualifies for the SOWP in 2026, how to apply correctly, what the current processing time is, and what happens if things go wrong along the way.

Who Is Eligible for the SOWP in 2026?

Eligibility for the SOWP through the spousal sponsorship process requires three conditions to be met simultaneously at the time of application.

Condition 1: An active PR sponsorship application. The principal applicant must be included in a spousal sponsorship permanent residence application that IRCC has confirmed is in processing. The confirmation is the Acknowledgment of Receipt (AOR). You cannot apply for the SOWP before the AOR arrives.

Condition 2: Physical presence in Canada. The sponsored person must be physically located in Canada at the time of applying for the SOWP. This requirement applies regardless of whether the sponsorship was filed as inland or outland.

Condition 3: Valid temporary resident status. The sponsored person must hold valid temporary status in Canada, such as a visitor record, work permit, or study permit, OR must have applied to extend or restore their status before it expired (maintained status).

All three conditions must be met at the same time. If one is missing, the SOWP application will be refused. The most common failure points are applying before the AOR arrives and allowing temporary status to expire before applying.

Critical distinction for 2026

The January 2025 IRCC restrictions on Spousal Open Work Permits do NOT apply to family class sponsorship applicants. Those restrictions reduced eligibility for spouses of foreign workers in lower-skilled TEER categories and most undergraduate student spouses. They have no effect on sponsored spouses and partners applying for permanent residence through family class. If you are being sponsored under spousal sponsorship, you remain fully eligible for the SOWP under IRCC’s public policy extended through December 31, 2026.

Inland vs Outland: Does Stream Choice Affect SOWP Eligibility?

Historically, only inland applicants could access the SOWP through the sponsorship process. A 2023 IRCC public policy changed this. Since May 2023, outland applicants who are physically present in Canada with valid temporary status can also apply for the SOWP. This policy has been extended and remains in effect through December 31, 2026.

In practical terms, a sponsored spouse who is in Canada on a visitor record and whose file is being processed as an outland (Family Class) application can still receive a SOWP after their AOR arrives. They get outland’s faster processing timeline, full travel flexibility, full IAD appeal rights if refused, AND work authorization during the wait. This combination makes the outland-from-within-Canada strategy one of the most advantageous approaches available in 2026.

One important distinction: outland applicants who are outside Canada cannot receive a SOWP through the sponsorship process. The permit requires physical presence in Canada. An outland applicant abroad would need to qualify for a separate work permit through another pathway if they need to work in Canada before their PR is approved.

The Correct LMIA Exemption Code: A74, Not A75

Using the correct LMIA exemption code is not a minor administrative detail. Using the wrong code is a common cause of SOWP refusals and processing delays that can be avoided entirely with the right information.

Code Who It Is For Notes
A74 Sponsored spouses and partners applying for PR under Family Class (spousal sponsorship) Use this code for all spousal sponsorship SOWP applications regardless of inland or outland stream
A75 Spouses of economic class PR applicants waiting for a Bridging Open Work Permit (BOWP) Wrong code for family class sponsorship — using A75 on a family class SOWP causes delays or refusal
C41 Spouses of skilled foreign workers in TEER 0 or 1 occupations Separate from spousal sponsorship — for spouses of temporary foreign workers
C42 Spouses of eligible international students Separate from spousal sponsorship — for spouses of students, with restrictions since January 2025

How to Apply for the SOWP: Step by Step

Step Action
1 Submit the complete spousal sponsorship PR application through IRCC’s Permanent Residence Portal.
2 IRCC conducts a completeness check. Every form must be present, signed, and accompanied by required documents. A single missing item triggers a return.
3 IRCC issues the Acknowledgment of Receipt (AOR), typically within 4 to 8 weeks of a complete submission. The AOR confirms the file is open and in processing.
4 Your spouse applies for the SOWP online using LMIA exemption code A74. They must be physically in Canada and hold valid temporary status (visitor record, work permit, or study permit) at the time of applying.
5 SOWP processing currently takes approximately 8 to 9 months in 2026. During this period, maintain status by applying for a visitor record extension 30 days before any existing status expires.
6 SOWP is issued. Your spouse can work for any employer in Canada immediately. No employer-specific job offer or LMIA is required.
7 If the SOWP approaches expiry while the PR application is still in process, apply for a two-year extension before the current permit expires.

SOWP Processing Time in 2026

SOWP processing times inside Canada have increased significantly in 2026. As of April 2026, IRCC’s published processing time for open work permits submitted from inside Canada is approximately 259 days, which is roughly 8.5 months. This is substantially longer than the 3 to 4 month timelines that applied in prior years.

What this means in practice: if you receive your AOR within 6 to 8 weeks of submitting the sponsorship application, and then apply for the SOWP immediately after, your spouse can expect to start working approximately 10 to 11 months after the initial sponsorship application was submitted. This is a significant wait. Planning your household finances around this realistic timeline is important.

The practical implication for stream selection: if your spouse needs to start working in Canada as early as possible and cannot wait 10 to 11 months, other options may be worth exploring alongside the SOWP application, including whether the sponsored person can maintain or extend an existing work permit through a different pathway until the SOWP is issued.

What Happens to Status While Waiting for the SOWP?

The sponsored person must maintain valid temporary resident status in Canada from the moment the sponsorship application is submitted until the SOWP is received. There is no automatic authorization to remain in Canada solely because a sponsorship application is in process, unless the person is already maintaining status through their own temporary permit.

If the sponsored person’s visitor record, work permit, or study permit is approaching expiry, apply for an extension at least 30 days before the current status expires. When an extension application is filed before the existing status expires, the person is in a period called maintained status and can legally remain in Canada and continue their current activities while the extension is being assessed. Do not allow status to lapse.

If status has already expired, IRCC has a two-week exception for urgent SOWP situations: if the AOR arrives and temporary status expires within 14 days, the SOWP application can still be submitted even before the AOR is technically confirmed. Seek professional guidance immediately if status has lapsed or is about to.

SOWP Extension: What to Do Before It Expires

If the SOWP is issued and the PR application is still in process as the permit’s expiry date approaches, a two-year extension is available. Apply for the extension before the SOWP expires. Do not wait until after expiry. As with all status documents in Canada, applying before expiry keeps the person in maintained status, allowing them to continue working while the extension is assessed.

The extension application uses the same A74 exemption code and follows the same process as the original SOWP application. Include updated documentation showing the PR application is still active and in process.

Can the SOWP Holder Change Employers?

Yes. An open work permit is not tied to a specific employer. The holder can change jobs, take a second job, switch industries, and work for any employer in Canada for any legal occupation during the permit’s validity period. They do not need to inform IRCC of an employer change during the validity period.

The SOWP does not, however, authorize the holder to become self-employed in certain regulated professions that require additional licensing or registration. If self-employment or a regulated profession is the goal, confirm the specific requirements for that profession in the relevant province before assuming the SOWP alone is sufficient.

Frequently Asked Questions

My spouse is outside Canada. Can they still get the SOWP?

The SOWP requires physical presence in Canada at the time of applying. If your spouse is outside Canada, they cannot apply for the SOWP through the sponsorship process. They would need to enter Canada first, establish valid temporary status, and then apply after the AOR arrives. If they are outside Canada and need to work in Canada during processing, explore whether they qualify for a separate work permit through another pathway.

We applied outland but my spouse is in Canada. Can they still get the SOWP?

Yes. As of 2023, IRCC’s public policy extends SOWP eligibility to outland applicants physically present in Canada with valid temporary status. This policy is in effect through December 31, 2026. Your spouse applies using LMIA exemption code A74 after receiving the AOR, exactly as an inland applicant would.

How long does the SOWP take to process in 2026?

As of April 2026, IRCC’s processing time for open work permits from inside Canada is approximately 259 days, or roughly 8.5 months. This is significantly longer than prior years. Plan household finances based on this realistic timeline rather than the 3 to 4 month timelines that were common before 2025.

What LMIA exemption code do I use?

Code A74 for family class spousal sponsorship applicants. Using A75 (which is for Bridging Open Work Permits in economic class) on a family class application causes processing delays or refusals. Double-check this before submitting.

Can my spouse work before the SOWP is issued?

Not unless they hold an existing valid work permit through another pathway. An AOR confirmation does not authorize work. Only an issued SOWP or a separate valid work permit authorizes employment. Never advise your spouse to begin working before their SOWP is in hand.
