Express Entry: Healthcare and Social Services Category

This category targets health and social services occupations such as nurses, physicians, dentists, psychologists, veterinarians and social workers, among others. To qualify, you need at least 12 months of work experience in an eligible occupation within the past three years, in Canada or abroad, and you must be eligible for an Express Entry program.

Licensing: The Step Many Applicants Underestimate

Most health professions are regulated by provincial bodies. You can often immigrate before you are licensed, but you cannot practise your profession until you meet provincial licensing requirements. Steps may include credential assessment, exams, supervised practice and language testing. Start early, because licensing can take months or longer.

  • Nurses: contact the provincial nursing regulator and start credential assessment.
  • Physicians: check provincial medical college pathways and practice-ready assessment options.
  • Allied health professionals: confirm the regulator and requirements in your target province.

Step-by-Step Plan for Health Care Workers

Step 1: Confirm your NOC and category eligibility

Match your duties to the correct NOC code and confirm you have 12 months of experience in the last three years.

Step 2: Strengthen your Express Entry profile

Raise language scores, order your ECA and prepare detailed reference letters.

Step 3: Begin licensing early

Start the provincial process in the province where you plan to work.

Step 4: Explore provincial streams

Register with provinces that prioritize health care and consider job offers from health authorities.

Step 5: Apply quickly after an invitation

Submit a complete application within 60 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it easy for healthcare workers to immigrate to Canada in 2026?

Health care workers have strong options, but nothing is guaranteed. In 2026, the Healthcare and Social Services category invited 11,500 candidates with cut-offs from 467 to 475, and physician draws had cut-offs as low as 169. Many provinces also prioritize health care. You still need to meet program and licensing requirements.

Do I need a Canadian licence before applying for PR?

Not always. Many health care workers can immigrate before they are licensed, but they cannot practise their regulated profession until they meet provincial requirements. Some provincial streams and employers require registration or licensing steps first. Start the licensing process early to avoid delays after arrival.

How much work experience do healthcare workers need for the category draw?

You need at least 12 months of work experience in an eligible healthcare or social services occupation within the past three years, gained in Canada or abroad. You must also meet the requirements of an Express Entry program, such as the Federal Skilled Worker Program or Canadian Experience Class.

Which provinces prioritize healthcare workers in 2026?

Many do. British Columbia’s “Care” priority covers 36 occupations, Alberta runs Dedicated Health Care Pathways, Saskatchewan lists health care as a priority sector, Ontario highlights nurses and physicians, and Nova Scotia has physician and health care graduate options. Requirements vary by stream.
