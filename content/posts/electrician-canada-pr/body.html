Quick Summary

Electricians (NOC 72200), industrial electricians (NOC 72201) and plumbers (NOC 72300) are part of the 2026 Express Entry trades category. Trades draws invited 3,000 candidates on April 1 at CRS 477 and 3,500 on October 1 at CRS 476. You need at least 12 months of experience in an eligible trade within the past three years. The Federal Skilled Trades Program, provincial trade priorities and Red Seal certification add further options.

The 2026 Express Entry Trades Category

In 2026, the trades category covers about 25 occupations focused on construction, installation and industrial trades. Key examples include electricians, industrial electricians, plumbers, carpenters, welders, heating, refrigeration and air conditioning mechanics, heavy-duty equipment mechanics and construction millwrights. Cooks and chefs were removed in February 2026.

The Federal Skilled Trades Program

The Federal Skilled Trades Program (FSTP) is designed for tradespeople. Key requirements include:

  • At least two years of full-time work experience in a skilled trade within the past five years.
  • Language ability of CLB 5 for speaking and listening and CLB 4 for reading and writing.
  • A valid full-time job offer of at least one year, or a certificate of qualification from a Canadian provincial or territorial authority.
  • Meeting the job requirements of the trade as set out in the NOC.

Certification: Why Red Seal Matters

Most electrical and plumbing work in Canada requires provincial certification. The Red Seal endorsement allows certified tradespeople to work across provinces. Steps usually include a trade qualification assessment, proof of experience and passing the certification exam. IRCC’s proposed 2026 Express Entry reforms included giving extra points for Red Seal certification. Until changes take effect, a Canadian certificate of qualification already adds skill transferability points when combined with language scores.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can electricians get PR in Canada in 2026?

Yes. Electricians and industrial electricians are part of the 2026 Express Entry trades category, which held draws on April 1 at CRS 477 and October 1 at CRS 476. They can also apply through the Federal Skilled Trades Program and provincial streams that prioritize trades, such as British Columbia’s “Build” priority.

Are plumbers eligible for Express Entry trades draws?

Yes. Plumbers (NOC 72300) are included in the 2026 trades category. You need at least 12 months of full-time experience in the trade within the past three years and must qualify for an Express Entry program. Provincial certification is required to work as a plumber in most provinces.

Do I need a Red Seal to immigrate as a tradesperson?

Not always for immigration, but certification is usually required to work. A Canadian certificate of qualification or a valid job offer is required for the Federal Skilled Trades Program. Certification combined with language scores also adds skill transferability points. IRCC has proposed future points for Red Seal certification.

Why were cooks removed from the trades category?

IRCC removed cooks and chefs from the trades category in February 2026 to refocus it on construction and industrial trades. Analysis of earlier draws showed cooks were taking a large share of trades invitations. The change gives electricians, plumbers and other construction trades more opportunities.
