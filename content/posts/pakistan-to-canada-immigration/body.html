Quick Summary

Pakistani citizens need a visitor visa to travel to Canada. Express Entry and provincial nominee programs are the main routes to permanent residence for skilled workers. A provincial nomination adds 600 CRS points. Strong English scores, an Educational Credential Assessment, detailed reference letters, proof of funds and police certificates are essential. French ability and category eligibility can significantly improve your chances in 2026.

Express Entry for Pakistani Applicants

Most Pakistani skilled workers apply through the Federal Skilled Worker Program, which requires at least one year of continuous skilled work experience, CLB 7 language ability, an ECA for foreign education, settlement funds and at least 67 points on the eligibility grid. Once in the pool, your Comprehensive Ranking System score decides your ranking.

How to raise your score

  • Reach CLB 9 or higher in English across all four abilities.
  • Learn French; NCLC 7 or higher adds 25 to 50 points and opens French category draws.
  • Claim all eligible credentials, including a second degree or diploma.
  • Include your spouse’s language and education if it helps your score.
  • Track categories such as healthcare, trades and STEM.

Study, Work and Family Pathways

  • Study: graduate students at public institutions are exempt from the provincial attestation letter since January 1, 2026; graduates may qualify for PGWPs and later PR.
  • Work: employer-supported work permits can build Canadian experience.
  • Family: citizens and permanent residents can sponsor spouses, partners and dependent children.

Reducing Refusal Risk

  • Make sure every detail is consistent across forms, letters and documents.
  • Explain gaps in employment or education honestly.
  • Declare previous refusals from any country.
  • Avoid agents who promise guaranteed outcomes or sell job offers.
  • Submit genuine documents only; misrepresentation can result in a five-year ban.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Pakistanis apply for Express Entry?

Yes. Pakistani citizens can apply through Express Entry if they meet program requirements, such as the Federal Skilled Worker Program’s work experience, language, education and points requirements. Candidates compete in the same pool as everyone else, so strong language scores and category eligibility matter.

Which province is best for Pakistani immigrants?

There is no single best province. Your best option depends on your occupation, job offer and connections. In 2026, Alberta, British Columbia and Saskatchewan prioritized health care, trades and selected technology roles, while Ontario launched a new employer-driven stream. Match your profile to each province’s priorities.

Do Pakistani citizens need a visa to visit Canada?

Yes. Pakistani citizens need a visitor visa (temporary resident visa) and must give biometrics. Applicants should show a clear purpose, ties to Pakistan and sufficient funds. A visitor visa does not allow work or long-term study in Canada.

How can Pakistani applicants improve their CRS score?

The most effective ways are raising English scores to CLB 9 or higher, learning French to NCLC 7 or higher, claiming all eligible credentials, including a spouse’s language and education where helpful, and pursuing a provincial nomination, which adds 600 points.
